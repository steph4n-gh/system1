# System1 1.2.0 release requirements

Approved 1 October 2026. Baseline: released 1.1.0, commit
`68ed82af5aa22b110b33a2fc1ebe2b1b309adb00`. Research protocols and source freezes
under `benchmarks/quality/release_1_2/` precede their evaluations.

## Supported release journey

Teach one text choice skill, correct explicit lessons, assess an isolated candidate,
optionally require independent qualification, explicitly adopt the exact approved
artifact, observe strict review on predictions, and restore a retained approved
revision. Expose the same behavior in Python, the command line, and the existing
local document workspace. Keep approved inference available during failed edits.

Keep existing session defaults, `system1`/`reflex` parity, saved-skill format v2,
and the NumPy/cryptography base dependencies. Reuse the existing optional logistic
compiler rather than add a solver. Legacy approved artifacts must remain usable;
active assessments must be regenerated after upgrading when their evidence schema
changes. Sessions remain local single-writer directories.

## Evidence and research decisions

Compare random, uncertain, and diverse uncertain lesson acquisition with equal total
inspection budgets. Compare the existing classifier, a conventional baseline, and
a bounded task-specific contrastive candidate. Select no new algorithm merely for
its novelty. Retain every frozen outcome, source restriction, failure, and measured
cost. Use prospective advancement rules in the experiment protocol.

Represent recurring assessment as development evidence. A separate qualification
cohort and policy are frozen before scoring and bound to the exact candidate.
Qualification inputs/groups cannot enter lessons or be reused for later candidates.
Two exact one-sided bounds share the specified confidence error budget. Such bounds
assume independent representative units; software cannot verify those assumptions,
source truth, or protection under arbitrary distribution shift.

Keep full-scope CLINC/BANKING77 requirements unchanged (99% accepted correctness,
80% supported coverage, at most 1% CLINC unfamiliar false acceptance, complete warm
p95 below 5 ms). New-source research screens do not qualify those workloads.

## Release gates

- Alpha: complete Python lifecycle, refusal paths, exact adoption and rollback.
- Beta: Python/CLI/workspace parity, stable public methods, migration and runnable
  instructions; bounded research and real-source workflow outcomes retained.
- Release candidate: full required tests, offline quality regression, documentation,
  lockfile, wheel/source contents, strict metadata, and clean installed-package
  checks. Optional coverage/skips remain explicit.
- Publication: push source and require green CI on the exact release SHA before
  tagging; verify publishing, published hashes, and independent installed behavior.

If lesson selection or classification experiments miss their predeclared advancement
rules, omit the proposed algorithm, retain the research, and assess whether the
remaining lifecycle features warrant the release. Do not lower a failed target.
